Live Photo

Video to Live Photo

Pick a video, choose any moment and length, and get a cover still plus a motion clip.

Works on your device. Nothing is uploaded.

The Motion Photo is one JPEG with the clip inside, which Google Photos and many Android phones play as a live photo. For an iPhone Live Photo, save the cover and clip and pair them with a free app such as intoLive, since Apple’s pairing metadata can’t be written from a browser.
